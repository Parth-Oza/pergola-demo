Frequently Asked Questions

How long does assembly take?
Most customers assemble with two people over a weekend. Professional installation is available in supported ZIP codes (check on the product page).
Does it really keep rain out?
Yes — with louvers closed, water is channeled into gutters hidden in the frame and drains down through the posts.
What surface do I need?
A level surface (concrete, pavers, or deck with proper footings) is required, especially if adding side walls.
How is it delivered?
Free freight delivery, typically 1–2 weeks in stock (2–4 weeks during peak periods). Large sizes may arrive as split shipments.
Can I add accessories later?
Yes — heaters, side walls, and RF controllers can all be added after the initial install.
